Common questions

Is my card data safe?
Yes — everything you enter (card nicknames, last 4 digits, reward rates) stays only on your device. Wallet Optimizer has no account system, no backend server, and doesn't transmit your data anywhere. See the privacy policy for details.

Why does Wallet Optimizer need my location?
Location is used on-device to figure out what kind of place you're at (a gas station, restaurant, etc.) so the app can recommend your best card for it. You can deny or revoke this anytime in iOS Settings — the app still works without it, you'll just need to pick your category manually.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app deletes everything, since nothing is stored anywhere else. You can also remove individual cards from inside the app.
